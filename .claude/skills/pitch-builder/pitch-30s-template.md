## Checklist
- [ ] ≤ 30.0 s (check with `ffprobe` or `uv run check-submission --demo`)
- [ ] English voice-over or English subtitles
- [ ] No NASA logos, flags, mission patches
- [ ] AI-generated visuals carry a visible "AI-generated" watermark
- [ ] Music/images licensed or own work, credited on the project page
- [ ] No minors' faces, voices or names
- [ ] Uploaded publicly (YouTube unlisted/public or Drive "anyone with link"), tested in a private window
