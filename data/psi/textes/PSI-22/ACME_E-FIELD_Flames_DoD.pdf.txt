To gain an initial understanding of this experiment, refer to the documents “ACME non-imaging data file 
description.pdf” and “E-FIELD Flames data interpretation document.pdf” located in the “Data Organization 
Document(s)” section of the “Scientific Data and Information” section on the “Data” tab.  
 
The E-FIELD Flames data is organization in PSI by co-flow burner position and mesh polarity, or power 
supply. Raw data, and Images & Videos data are available on the “Scientific Data and Information Tab” of 
each of the four “Experiment Data” records:  
 
• Co-Flow Burner – Negative Power Supply 
• Co-Flow Burner – Positive Power Supply 
• Gas-Jet Burner – Negative Power Supply 
• Gas-Jet Burner – Positive Power Supply 
 
 
Within each “Experiment Data” record, “Raw Data”, “Images” and “Audio/Video Files” are available on the 
“Scientific Data and Information” tab. Data records are identified by year (2 digits), day of year (GMT, 3 
digits), a letter (1 character) and a number (1 digit) – where the different numbers can be for repeats but 
are typically variants, e.g., with different flow rate(s) or instrumentation setting(s), e.g. 18073A3. Record 
naming and listings are based on the detailed information in the test conditions table below (page 2). 
 
Test specific raw data files are located on the “Raw Data and Information” tab of the “Raw & Analyzed 
Data” record. The headers of the basic data in .csv files is explained in Section 10.1, beginning on page 
126 of the “ACME-DOC-010 Rev D.pdf” located in the “Data Organization Document(s)” section of the 
“Scientific Data and Information” section on the “Data” tab of the ACME E-FIELD Flames “Investigations” 
record. Associated videos are located on the “General” tab and associated images are located on the 
“Image Data and Information” tab of the “Videos & Images” record. 
 
There are various other supporting engineering documents, videos, reports and publications available on 
the other tabs of the “Investigations” record.
