Impacts, Benefits, and Products 
This text is the ‘Impacts, Benefits, and Products’ section from the 2019 version of the final report. 
 
The impact of this work is, as the original objectives identified, the delivery of a unique data set of chemi-
ion behavior in flames under sufficiently stable and symmetric conditions that models and theories can 
be evaluated against the results.  As an example, the very distinctive shape of the V-I curve in microgravity, 
when matched in models, will give a very good indication that the feedback between ion driven wind and 
changes in flame shape and burning behavior that then further change the ion current is captured 
accurately. 
 
These results have already garnered the interest of other groups working on electric field effects on 
flames.  
 
The use of electric field effects on flames can be as sensor (variations of ion current as associated with 
combustion behavior) or actuator (ion -driven wind changes the flame shape and characteristics).  This 
work, as was originally planned, provides quantitative evaluation of the sensing and response potential of 
electric fields in combustion.  The applications can include the control of flames. 
 
Patents 
Thus far, no patents have resulted from the E-FIELD Flames research.
