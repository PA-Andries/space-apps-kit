# Whiteboard: ideas and display sketches (Sat 14 Nov 2026)

Transcription of `whiteboard-display-ideas.png`, a photo of the team's whiteboard given by PA, for agents that cannot view images. French words are kept as written on the board, with a translation. This is a sketch of ideas, not a decision: `DECISIONS.md` and `BRIEF_EQUIPE_IA.md` stay the reference.

Not transcribed: a small note in the top-left corner that is not legible.

## Top row: the three objectives of the statement

1. Identify investigations most relevant [to] fire safety needs.
2. Extract data for predictive modelling.
3. Highlight research for future fire safety.

## Left: what the user gives

- **Habitable crew environment**, one of: spacecraft, pressurized rover, space stations, lunar and Mars bases.
- **Data**, with an arrow pointing up to the habitable crew environment, made of four axes: g (gravity), O2, P (pressure), combustible (fuel).

## Middle: the flow

The environment and its data point to **Investigations**, numbered 1, 2, 3, 4 ... 24 (the 24 investigations).

An arrow goes from the investigations to a box listing what is shown for each one:

- comportement (behaviour)
- dangers
- prevention
- trace de donnée, pas encore fait (data trace, not done yet)
- comparaison (comparison)

## Right, top: display sketch 1

A screen with:

- "Titre" (title) at the top.
- Four cards in a 2 × 2 grid, top left.
- Sliders on the right, labelled O2, g, P and combustible, with dots above them suggesting more could be added.
- A chart with P on the vertical axis and O2 on the horizontal axis. Points are drawn as crosses; several groups of crosses are circled, one of them inside a rectangle near the origin. A circled "g" sits next to the origin (gravity as a third dimension or a selector; the board does not say which).
- A button "ANALYSE" under the chart.
- A "chatbot" icon in the bottom-right corner.

## Right, bottom: display sketch 2 (marked "Pas encore fait", not done yet)

A screen with:

- Three boxes in a row at the top: the three selected items. Their labels are not clearly legible (the second and third are ditto marks of the first).
- "comparaison" (comparison) under the three boxes.
- "Synthèse" (synthesis): comportement du feu (fire behaviour), inflammabilité (flammability), dangers, prévention (prevention), détection (detection).
- An icon labelled "modèle de feu" (fire model), to the right of the synthesis.
- A "chatbot" icon in the bottom-right corner.
