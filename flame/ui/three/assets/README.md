# NASA images used by the 3D scenes

All files are NASA images, in the public domain in the United States. They are credited on the home page.

| File | What it is | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `earth.jpg`, `earth_1k.jpg` | Blue Marble, cloud-free mosaic of the Earth (2048 and 1024 px wide) | NASA Scientific Visualization Studio, https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/2915 (`bluemarble-2048.png`) |
| `moon.jpg`, `moon_1k.jpg` | Moon colour map from the Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter camera (LROC WAC) | NASA SVS CGI Moon Kit, https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720 (`lroc_color_2k.jpg`) |
| `mars.jpg`, `mars_1k.jpg` | Global colour map of Mars | NASA 3D Resources, https://github.com/nasa/NASA-3D-Resources (`Images and Textures/Mars`) |

Changes: re-encoded as JPEG (quality 72 to 80) and reduced to 1024 px for the `_1k` versions.
The station, the spacecraft and the pressurized rover are simplified shapes drawn in `places.html`, not NASA models.
