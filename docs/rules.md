# Rules summary

Compiled 12 Oct 2026 from the official site. Items marked (2025) come from last year's Project Submission Guide; the 2026 guide is published on 13 Nov, so re-check them then.

## Event

- NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026, theme "The Next Frontier", 14-15 Nov 2026, Paris local event.
- Submission opens Sat 14 Nov 09:00 and closes **Sun 15 Nov 23:59 Paris time**. Final, no edits after.
- Free. Teams of 1-6. All members registered and confirmed at the **same local event**. No additions after the deadline.
- Under-18s must be registered by and accompanied by a parent/guardian.

## Eligibility for global judging

- Must answer an **official 2026 challenge**. "Create your own challenge" is not eligible for global awards.
- Must use **NASA data or resources**. Space-agency partner data and other open data are allowed on top.
- All required project-page fields complete, all content in English.

## Project page fields (2025)

1. Project name
2. High-level project summary
3. Link to project demo
4. Link to final project
5. Detailed project description (what it does, how, benefits, goals, tools/languages/hardware/software)
6. NASA data (which, how used)
7. Space agency partner and other data / resources (all code, text, images used)
8. Use of artificial intelligence
9. Submission verification (terms + originality)

## Demo (2025)

- **Video ≤ 30 seconds** OR **slides ≤ 7** including the title slide. Over the limit = ineligible for global judging.
- Hosted externally (YouTube, Drive, GitHub...), publicly accessible.
- English, or English subtitles for the video.
- Official video beats: team (0:00), challenge and inspiration (0:02), title and tagline (0:05), how it works and data used (0:10), impact (0:20-0:30).

## Final project link

Public, no password, permission or registration: website, deployed app or code repository.

## Use of AI (2025 guide, official wording summarised)

- **AI tools are permitted.** You must state which tools you used and how, in the "Use of Artificial Intelligence" field.
- **Images and videos** generated by AI must carry a **visible watermark** saying they are AI-generated.
- **Audio** generated by AI: acknowledge it in the description text and metadata.
- **Code and data** generated by AI: acknowledge it in the description text and metadata.
- AI-generated content may **not use, contain or modify NASA branding** (logos, flags, mission identifiers).
- Judges "will consider originality, intent, and execution": heavy AI use is fine if the idea and execution are yours and it's declared.
- The Participant FAQ and Terms don't add other AI restrictions.

## Ownership and licence (2026 Participant Terms)

- Original content must be created for the challenge and **licensed as open source** (OSI-approved). Otherwise NASA distributes it under **Apache 2.0**.
- You must hold rights to everything you submit, credit every source, and only use non-NASA material that is freely available or open source.
- No promotion of commercial products or services; participants are not paid.

## Content restrictions

No offensive or illegal content, no commercial promotion, no proprietary/confidential info, no personal data, no recognisable minors, adults featured must consent to NASA use.
