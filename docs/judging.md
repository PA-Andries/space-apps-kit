# Judging

## Criteria (equal attention, no published weights)

| Criterion | Judges ask | How to score high |
|---|---|---|
| Influence | How much positive impact? Big or small problem? | Name the user, quantify who benefits, show a local case |
| Creativity | How novel vs existing solutions? | Name what exists and the angle that's new |
| Validity | Scientifically sound, workable in the real world? | Show method, assumptions, limits, a sanity check against known values |
| Relevance | Answers the challenge? Complete, feasible, usable? | Map features to the statement's objectives one by one; working deployed demo |
| Presentation | Tells the story: challenge, solution, why it matters? | 30-second video with real product footage, clear project page |

## Process

1. **Local (Paris)**: local judges choose local winners (local prizes, if any) and the teams that advance to global judging. These can differ.
2. **Global**: NASA and partner experts pick Global Finalists (45 in 2025) and Honorable Mentions. Announced December 2026.
3. **Executive**: a committee picks the 10 Global Winners, one per award. Announced January 2027. Winners are invited to a celebration with NASA (2024: NASA HQ + Goddard).

## The 10 global awards

| Award | Rewards |
|---|---|
| Best Use of Science | Most valid, effective use of the scientific method |
| Best Use of Data | Making space data more accessible or using it in a unique way |
| Best Use of Technology | Most innovative use of technology |
| Galactic Impact | Most potential to improve life on Earth or beyond |
| Best Mission Concept | Most plausible concept and design |
| Most Inspirational | Moves the judges the most |
| Best Storytelling | Creative communication of open data's potential |
| Global Connection | Technology that best connects people worldwide |
| Art & Technology | Best blend of technical and creative skills |
| Local Impact | Greatest potential to make a difference in a local community |

## 2025 global winners (patterns)

| Award | Project | What |
|---|---|---|
| Science | SpaceGenes+ | Dashboard on radiation + microgravity effects at molecular level |
| Data | Resonant Exoplanets | AI scanning telescope data incl. JWST spectra |
| Technology | Twisters / SkySense | NASA EO data + AI for hyperlocal forecasts and safest activity times |
| Galactic Impact | Astro Sweepers | Debris compliance reports and risk score for tracked objects |
| Mission Concept | PureFlow | 3D habitat design tool tested against solar storms |
| Inspirational | Photonics Odyssey | Satellite internet as national infrastructure |
| Storytelling | HerCode Space | Illustrated children's book on space weather |
| Global Connection | Gaia+LEO | Orbital + ground data-centre network optimisation |
| Art & Tech | Zumorroda-X | Mini-games about farmers adapting to climate |
| Local Impact | QUEÑARIS | Satellite + drones + AI to restore forests protecting a city's water |

Patterns: real NASA data + a concrete user + a clear outcome. Non-code formats (book, games) can win when they fit the award. Three winners came from the same challenge, each with a distinct angle.
